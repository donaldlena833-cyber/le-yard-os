# Le Yard Marketing

Create restrained, specific hospitality marketing that is ready for human review and channel adaptation.

## Establish the brief

Confirm the objective, audience, channel, geography, timing, offer, call to action, success measure, required assets, and approval owner. Use current canonical public sources for business identity, address, hours, reservation behavior, and availability. Treat a user-approved campaign decision as campaign evidence; treat everything else as unknown until verified.

## Create the work

1. Build a claim ledger before drafting: claim, source, retrieval date, approval status, and channels where it may appear.
2. Write one core message, then adapt it to each channel's real format and constraints. Keep voice elegant, concrete, and unforced; avoid generic luxury language.
3. Preserve accessibility, alt-text needs, link destinations, legal lines, audience exclusions, and consent requirements.
4. When imagery is needed, use approved assets or create clearly labeled concepts. Do not represent a render, mockup, or generated scene as documentary photography.
5. Return a review-ready brief, channel drafts, asset list, claim ledger, and preflight checklist.

## Public/private boundary

- Use only approved public facts in released copy. Do not expose private founder material, lease or filing details, supplier negotiations, unannounced finances, staff data, guest data, operational incidents, or commercial uncertainty.
- Do not use a private source merely because it is connected. Ask whether the fact is approved for public release.
- Never narrate private relationship or personnel changes as marketing context unless the user explicitly approves the exact public wording.

## Release boundary

A draft, approved draft, scheduled post, sent email, published page, indexed page, and measured result are distinct states. Do not send, schedule, publish, modify a live profile, or claim performance without explicit authorization and visible confirmation. If a channel requires login, CAPTCHA, identity confirmation, or a missing approval, stop and present the prepared artifact and blocker.
