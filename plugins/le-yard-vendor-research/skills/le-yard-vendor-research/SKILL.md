# Le Yard Vendor Research

Build a decision-ready supplier comparison while preserving the line between evidence, vendor claims, estimates, outreach, and completed commercial actions.

## Establish the requirement

Capture the product or service specification, acceptable substitutions, quantity and pack needs, delivery destination and window, budget or target range, required certifications, visual requirements, and decision date. Proceed with clearly labeled low-risk assumptions when possible; ask only when a missing choice would materially change the shortlist.

## Research and normalize

1. Use current sources. Prefer manufacturer or supplier pages, current catalogs, dated quotes, and authoritative certification records. Preserve direct URLs and observation dates.
2. Keep one row per supplier and exact product combination. Deduplicate trading names, resellers, and repeated listings without merging distinct factories or products.
3. Record product identity, material and dimensions, pack size, MOQ, unit and landed-cost components, sample terms, lead time, delivery constraints, payment terms, returns, certifications, and photo provenance.
4. Normalize currencies, quantities, and packs without hiding the original quoted form. Separate product price, freight, duty, tax, setup, and recurring charges.
5. Label each field as verified evidence, vendor-claimed, estimated, conflicting, stale, or unavailable. Missing evidence is unavailable, never zero.
6. Score fit only against the stated criteria. Show the weighting and keep confidence separate from the numeric score.

## Deliverable

Return:

- a concise requirements summary;
- an evidence matrix with supplier, exact product, source and date, specification fit, MOQ or pack, normalized cost, lead time, sample status, and risks;
- a ranked shortlist with the reason each option survives;
- unresolved questions and the next approval needed;
- a source list linking every decision-driving claim.

## Commercial boundaries

- A discovered listing, copied URL, drafted message, visible chat, supplier reply, accepted quote, sample order, purchase order, and payment are different states. Never collapse them.
- Draft outreach unless the user explicitly authorizes sending. Before any send, verify the supplier identity, recipient, message, and active composer; stop for login, CAPTCHA, identity mismatch, missing composer, or failed confirmation.
- Never claim an order, sample, quote acceptance, or payment without visible confirmation from the relevant system.
- Do not place orders, accept terms, transfer money, or expose credentials or private personal data without separate explicit authorization.
