# Le Yard Premises Admin

Turn a document set into a reliable control surface for owners and professional advisers.

## Build the source record

Inventory every source by title, parties or issuing authority, execution or issue date, version, status, and page count. Treat signed documents and authoritative agency records as controlling over amendments, drafts, email summaries, notes, or memory. Preserve superseded material but label it.

For current legal, regulatory, permit, license, fee, form, or deadline questions, verify against the responsible government or agency source and include the retrieval date. State when an authoritative source is unavailable or ambiguous.

## Extract obligations and issues

For each item, capture:

- exact page, section, exhibit, or official-source citation;
- responsible party and required action;
- fixed date or triggering event, notice window, dependency, and renewal cadence;
- amount or formula exactly as written, without silently completing blanks;
- required evidence, reviewer, current status, and next decision;
- conflicts, missing exhibits, unsigned pages, undefined terms, and professional-review questions.

Reconcile amendments against the base document in effective-date order. Do not combine conflicting terms into a guessed answer.

## Deliverable

Return a source inventory, executive issue list, obligation matrix, deadline calendar, missing-document request list, and decision log. Separate facts, calculations, interpretations, and open questions. Cite every decision-driving statement.

## Authority boundary

- This workflow prepares evidence and questions; it does not provide legal, tax, architectural, engineering, or licensing advice.
- Never sign, accept terms, file, certify, submit, pay, schedule an inspection, or message a counterparty without explicit authorization and final review.
- A draft, assembled packet, uploaded file, scheduled appointment, filed submission, accepted filing, issued approval, and paid fee are distinct states. Report only the state proven by evidence.
- Keep private documents in the user-supplied or approved connected location. Never add leases, IDs, signatures, credentials, or live filing packets to this public plugin repository.
